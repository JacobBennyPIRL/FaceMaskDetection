During the COVID-19 pandemic, one of the most challenging hurdles was to control the corona virus from spread- ing and keep the number of individuals being affected at bay. As the number of infected cases increased, the healthcare resources became scarce making it a predicament for the authorities and healthcare providers. Due to the highly contagious and airborne nature of the virus, many nations have mandated the use of a face mask in public and crowded spaces as a preventive measure to minimize the virus from transmission. However, it is a strenuous task for the authorities to monitor huge masses to identify the individuals who do not wear the face mask and encourage the use of the same. This is where deep learning can play a vital role to ease the process of distinguishing people who wears a face mask from those who do not wear it. The aim of this project is to use deep learning techniques to recognize the face mask on individuals and report whether the person is wearing a face mask or not. For model implementation, a collaboration of Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) and ResNet architectures using transfer learning will be used. This fusion aims to increase the accuracy and robustness of face mask detection in a variety of real-world scenarios, addressing the critical need for effective public health initiatives. In vision techniques like this, image recognition and classification are the most crucial tasks, to train the model for achieving higher accuracy thus attaining the desired results.
